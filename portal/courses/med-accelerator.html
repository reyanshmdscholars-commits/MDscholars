Course Overview

Accelerates the research productivity of medical students at any year of training. Pairs each scholar with a physician-investigator and a real dataset, with the goal of producing a submitted manuscript or conference presentation that strengthens residency applications.

Course Goals

  • Translate a clinical observation into a publishable research question
  • Execute a complete dataset-based analysis end-to-end
  • Author a manuscript draft for peer-reviewed submission
  • Build a residency-application–ready scholarly profile
  • Establish mentorship that extends beyond the program

What You'll Walk Away With

Research protocol IRB-aware data plan Statistical analysis + figures Conference abstract (submitted) Manuscript draft (IMRAD) ERAS-ready scholarly profile Mentor evaluation letter

Expectations

  • Time commitment: Plan for 6–10 hours per week of mentored work, reading, and assignments.
  • Assignment cadence: One short written deliverable per week (max 1000 words), submitted via the Submit Assignment tab.
  • Mentor meetings: Regular 1:1 or small-group sessions with your assigned physician mentor.
  • Authorship policy: Authorship is ICMJE-compliant and earned, not gifted. We document your contributions to support any publications that arise from the program.
  • Group work: Some assignments allow group submissions when you're on a team. Use the toggle on the assignment form to choose individual or group submission.
Tip — locked weeks become available as you complete prior assignments.
