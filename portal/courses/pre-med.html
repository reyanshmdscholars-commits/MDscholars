Course Overview

Equips undergraduate pre-med students with the practical research skills and AMCAS-ready scholarly output medical schools care about. Students execute a real dataset-based project from research question to submitted abstract, paired with a physician mentor.

Course Goals

  • Develop a publishable research question grounded in current literature
  • Conduct hands-on analysis of a de-identified clinical dataset
  • Apply descriptive and inferential biostatistics to a real research problem
  • Write a structured abstract using IMRAD
  • Document research activity in AMCAS-ready format
  • Submit work to a regional or national conference

What You'll Walk Away With

Formal research question + PICO Literature review (mini-systematic) Dataset analysis portfolio (SPSS / R / STATA output) Conference-ready abstract AMCAS scholarly activity writeup Mentor evaluation letter Possible co-authorship on a manuscript

Expectations

  • Time commitment: Plan for 6–10 hours per week of mentored work, reading, and assignments.
  • Assignment cadence: One short written deliverable per week (max 1000 words), submitted via the Submit Assignment tab.
  • Mentor meetings: Regular 1:1 or small-group sessions with your assigned physician mentor.
  • Authorship policy: Authorship is ICMJE-compliant and earned, not gifted. We document your contributions to support any publications that arise from the program.
  • Group work: Some assignments allow group submissions when you're on a team. Use the toggle on the assignment form to choose individual or group submission.
Tip — locked weeks become available as you complete prior assignments.
