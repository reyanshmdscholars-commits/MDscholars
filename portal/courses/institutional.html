Course Overview

A customized version of the Pre-Med / Future-Physician programs delivered to a partner school, college, or hospital system. Includes group projects tied to the institution's clinical interests, institutional research-day presentation, and bulk certification.

Course Goals

  • Train an institutional cohort on shared research methodology
  • Produce institution-aligned research outputs
  • Build a sustainable peer mentorship culture within the partner site
  • Hold an internal Research Day for the cohort
  • Generate publishable outputs aligned with institutional priorities

What You'll Walk Away With

Cohort research projects (group or individual) Internal Research Day presentations Submitted conference abstracts Bulk MD Scholars Completion Certificates Institutional mentorship-program playbook

Expectations

  • Time commitment: Plan for 6–10 hours per week of mentored work, reading, and assignments.
  • Assignment cadence: One short written deliverable per week (max 1000 words), submitted via the Submit Assignment tab.
  • Mentor meetings: Regular 1:1 or small-group sessions with your assigned physician mentor.
  • Authorship policy: Authorship is ICMJE-compliant and earned, not gifted. We document your contributions to support any publications that arise from the program.
  • Group work: Some assignments allow group submissions when you're on a team. Use the toggle on the assignment form to choose individual or group submission.
Tip — locked weeks become available as you complete prior assignments.
