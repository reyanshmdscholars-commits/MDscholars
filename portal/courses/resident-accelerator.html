Course Overview

The Pre-Med Research Scholars program is a 12-week, faculty-mentored research experience designed for undergraduate students preparing to apply to medical school. You'll work on a real, dataset-based research project alongside a physician mentor, build the technical skills that AMCAS reviewers care about, and produce a conference-ready abstract.

Course Goals

  • Develop a working research question and the methodology to answer it
  • Gain hands-on experience with de-identified clinical datasets
  • Learn descriptive and inferential biostatistics relevant to clinical research
  • Produce a complete abstract (background, methods, results, conclusion) submitted to a regional or national conference
  • Acquire AMCAS-ready documentation of your scholarly activity

What You'll Walk Away With

📄 Conference abstract 📊 Dataset analysis portfolio 📝 AMCAS-ready writeup 🏅 Research Completion Certificate 👨‍⚕️ Physician mentor evaluation letter 📑 Possible co-authorship

Expectations

  • Time commitment: Plan for 8–10 hours per week of mentored work, reading, and project development.
  • Assignment cadence: One short written deliverable per week (max 1000 words), submitted via the Submit Assignment tab.
  • Mentor meetings: One 30-minute video meeting per week with your assigned physician mentor.
  • Authorship policy: Authorship is ICMJE-compliant and earned, not gifted. We document your contributions to support any publications that arise from the program.
