Course Overview

A deep, year-long mentored research apprenticeship for committed students at the high-school through pre-med level. Six modules over twelve months take a student from research literacy to first-author conference presentation and peer-reviewed manuscript submission. Designed for students serious about science and medicine.

Course Goals

  • Develop a sophisticated, mentor-vetted research question
  • Become fluent in clinical research methodology and biostatistics
  • Execute a complete research project from protocol to manuscript
  • Submit work to a national conference and a peer-reviewed journal
  • Build a long-term research mentorship relationship
  • Graduate with a polished CV and references for college / med-school applications

What You'll Walk Away With

Quarterly progress portfolios Two submitted abstracts (one regional, one national) A peer-reviewed manuscript submission Statistical methods notebook (R / SPSS) MD Scholars 12-Month Completion Certificate Mentor evaluation letter for college / med school Possible first-author or co-author publication

Expectations

  • Time commitment: Plan for 6–10 hours per week of mentored work, reading, and assignments.
  • Assignment cadence: One short written deliverable per week (max 1000 words), submitted via the Submit Assignment tab.
  • Mentor meetings: Regular 1:1 or small-group sessions with your assigned physician mentor.
  • Authorship policy: Authorship is ICMJE-compliant and earned, not gifted. We document your contributions to support any publications that arise from the program.
  • Group work: Some assignments allow group submissions when you're on a team. Use the toggle on the assignment form to choose individual or group submission.
Tip — locked weeks become available as you complete prior assignments.
