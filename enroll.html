Pricing & Discounts

Track-specific pricing is provided to applicants upon acceptance. The following discount and payment-plan structure applies across all tracks:

Early Pay-in-Full Discount

Save $150 – $200 when you pay the full course fee in advance, right after acceptance. The exact discount is communicated in your acceptance email and depends on the track.

2-Payment Plan (no discount)

Split your tuition into two equal installments:

  • Payment 1 — at the time of application acceptance
  • Payment 2 — by the end of the 2nd week of the course

No discount applies to the 2-payment plan.

3-Payment Plan

Available for 12-month and extended programs. Spread payments over three installments to fit your budget and cash flow.

Refund Policy

Full Refund — Week 1

If you decide the course isn't right for you during the first week of class, we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.

50% Refund — Week 2

If you withdraw during the second week of class, you're entitled to a 50% refund.

No Refund — After Week 2

Refunds are not available after the 2nd week of the course start date.

Your Data is Secure

MD Scholars never stores payment data. Bank transfer details are encrypted in transit and confirmed against your enrollment by our bookkeeping system before being archived.

When card / Apple Pay / Google Pay launches, all transactions will be processed through a PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processor — we will not see or store your full payment details.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do I pay?

Payment is due upon enrollment confirmation. If you select a payment plan, you'll receive an invoice for the first installment immediately, with subsequent payments due on agreed dates. You'll have 48 hours to complete payment before your spot is released.

What if I need to cancel?

We offer a Week 1 full refund policy from the date of enrollment. After that, please contact us to discuss deferment to a future cohort or a partial refund, depending on timing and circumstances.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes! MD Scholars offers need-based scholarships and payment plans for qualified applicants. During the application process, you can request financial assistance. Our team will work with you to make the program accessible.
