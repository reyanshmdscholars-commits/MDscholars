Resident, IMG & Fellow Accelerator

ACGME-aligned scholarship support for residents and fellows who need to meet scholarly activity requirements while building a fellowship-competitive or academic faculty-level portfolio. International Medical Graduates (IMGs) pursuing US residency benefit substantially from this track — peer-reviewed publications and conference abstracts carry significant weight in residency program rankings, often distinguishing IMG applicants from the broader pool.

🕐 Ongoing enrollment
👥 Small cohort (6–10 scholars)
📍 Remote-first, flexible scheduling

Program Overview

The Resident, IMG & Fellow Accelerator is designed for the unique time constraints and scholarly requirements of GME training. Working with physician-scientist mentors, trainees develop manuscripts from existing clinical datasets, meet ACGME scholarly activity documentation requirements, and build research portfolios competitive for fellowship applications and academic appointments.

For International Medical Graduates (IMGs)

Research output is one of the strongest differentiators in the US residency match. Programs use peer-reviewed publications, conference abstracts, and case reports as a proxy for academic potential — and many top programs explicitly require or strongly weight research productivity. IMGs without US clinical experience can use scholarly output to demonstrate analytical rigor, English-language scientific communication, and engagement with the US research community. This track is structured to produce that output efficiently while you continue working clinically.

Curriculum Modules

  • 1

    Scholarly Activity Audit & Planning

    Review of ACGME requirements for your specialty, current scholarly standing, and 12-month research roadmap.

  • 2

    Dataset Access & Project Matching

    Matching with active research projects and datasets relevant to your specialty — no need to start from scratch.

  • 3

    Advanced Biostatistics & Analysis

    Practical statistical support including multivariate regression, survival analysis, and propensity score methodology.

  • 4

    Manuscript Development Pipeline

    From data to draft to submission — full manuscript development with senior author physician oversight.

  • 5

    Peer Review & Journal Strategy

    Target journal selection, cover letter writing, peer review response strategy, and resubmission coaching.

  • 6

    ACGME Documentation & Fellowship Portfolio

    Formal scholarly activity documentation, fellowship research narrative support, and academic CV development.

Deliverables & Outcomes

Resident & Fellow Accelerator participants receive ACGME-compliant scholarly activity documentation, at minimum one peer-reviewed manuscript submission, dataset-based research deliverables, and a fellowship application research narrative. Ongoing mentorship is available for those pursuing academic faculty careers.

Program Courses

Every accepted student walks away with concrete, portfolio-ready scholarly work — not just seat time. Here's what enrolled students complete during the program:

By the end of this program, students may complete:

  • Rapid-cycle research question + protocol
  • Systematic review search strategy OR QI driver diagram
  • Statistical analysis + figures
  • Conference abstract (submitted)
  • Manuscript draft (IMRAD or PRISMA)

Enrolled students access full weekly modules, templates, and mentor-guided assignments in the portal after acceptance. Deliverables vary based on progress, mentor guidance, and each student's chosen research question.
