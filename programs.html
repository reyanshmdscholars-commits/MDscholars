Pricing & Enrollment

Pricing is available upon acceptance. Apply or contact us to receive your personalized enrollment offer based on your program track, cohort size, and institutional needs.

Tuition & Refund Policy

Transparent pricing. No hidden fees.

Pay in Full (early-bird)

$150–$200 off when you pay the full course tuition in advance.

Two Installments

Half on acceptance, half by Week 2 of the course. No discount; same total cost.

Refund Window

Week 1: full refund · Week 2: 50% · After Week 2: no refund.

How to pay

We accept ACH bank transfer and mailed check / bank draft. Full payment details (ACH routing and account, mailing address, memo instructions) are sent privately in your acceptance email so we can match your payment to your enrollment.

Full payment instructions are emailed once your application is accepted. Questions: support@mdscholars.com
