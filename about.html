It's Really About You

At MD Scholars, everything we do is centered around you — your goals, your curiosity, and your journey toward a career in medicine or science.

Mission & Vision

Training the Next Generation of Scholars

MD Scholars is dedicated to cultivating the next generation of scholars by providing rigorous academic preparation, research mentorship, communication skills, and professional development for aspiring medical leaders.

Research Advisors

DvS

Dr. Divya Subramaniam, PhD, MPH

Director of Scientific Communication & Publications

Patient-centered outcomes research · qualitative methodology · scientific communications

Read bio

Dr. Subramaniam is a research scientist and scientific communications leader with 12 years of experience designing, conducting, and translating complex health research into actionable evidence for patients, clinicians, payers, and regulators. She specializes in patient-centered outcomes research, qualitative research methodology, and translating research findings into compelling scientific narratives.

Throughout her career, Dr. Subramaniam has led rigorous research across oncology, chronic disease, palliative care, and vaccines. She has authored and co-authored 32+ peer-reviewed publications and led 70+ scientific presentations at major conferences. Her work demonstrates a deep commitment to incorporating patient voice into research and drug development through qualitative methods including concept elicitation, cognitive interviews, and focus groups.

As a former Associate Professor, Dr. Subramaniam directed a portfolio of 15–20 concurrent research projects while mentoring 20+ junior researchers and graduate students. She built and scaled six graduate programs in health data science and health outcomes research, generating $5.6M in revenue. She currently operates a consulting practice advising pharmaceutical and biotech sponsors on patient-centered research strategy and scientific communications.

When she's not working on research, Dr. Subramaniam loves reading, traveling, and adventuring with her English Pointer, Benson.

Dr. Dipti P. Subramaniam, PhD, MPH

Scientific Research Advisor

Associate Medical Director, IntegrityCE · HEOR/RWE · scientific publications

Read bio

Dr. Dipti P. Subramaniam is a PhD-trained public health scientist and health outcomes researcher with more than 10 years of experience in academia, research, curriculum development, and scientific communication. She currently serves as an Associate Medical Director at IntegrityCE, LLC, where she applies her expertise in HEOR/RWE, scientific publications, and adult learning to the development of evidence-based continuing medical education programs.

Prior to joining IntegrityCE, she spent nearly a decade as a professor, where she led research and curriculum initiatives and developed research programs for medical students, providing learners with hands-on experience in research methodology and scientific writing. Throughout her academic career, she mentored learners across the training continuum, from undergraduate and medical students to graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and clinical faculty.

Her scholarship spans oncology, chronic diseases, women's health, and vaccination behavior, with more than 20 peer-reviewed publications and presentations at national scientific meetings. She is also an active peer reviewer, Special Topics Editor at Frontiers, and a member of the American Medical Writers Association.
