Medical Student Track

Medical Student Accelerator

Strengthen your residency application with peer-reviewed publications and scholarly presentations. Designed to fit into MS1–MS4 timelines without overwhelming your clinical schedule.

12-week intensive
Small cohort (6–10 scholars)
Remote-first, flexible scheduling

Program Overview

The Medical Student Accelerator bridges the gap between clinical training and scholarly productivity. With ERAS deadlines in mind, students work 1:1 with physician mentors to develop manuscripts, submit to national conferences, and build a research portfolio that makes them competitive for their specialty of choice.

Curriculum Modules

  • 1

    Research Strategy Session

    Personalized assessment of research interests, career goals, and timeline — building your scholarly roadmap.

  • 2

    Advanced Study Design

    Case-control, cohort, meta-analysis, systematic review — in-depth methodology relevant to specialty of interest.

  • 3

    IRB & Ethics in Clinical Research

    Practical overview of IRB submission, informed consent frameworks, and research ethics in clinical settings.

  • 4

    Manuscript Development Workshop

    Section-by-section manuscript development with iterative faculty feedback across multiple writing cycles.

  • 5

    National Conference Submission

    Abstract submission to specialty-specific national conferences (e.g., ACS, AHA, SGIM) with coaching for oral/poster presentation.

  • 6

    ERAS Documentation & Letter Support

    Research activity documentation for ERAS and performance-based letter of support from physician mentor.

Deliverables & Outcomes

Medical Student Accelerator graduates receive ERAS-ready research documentation, a national conference abstract submission, manuscript submission or in-revision status, and a performance-based letter of support from their physician mentor. All authorship follows ICMJE criteria.

Program Details

Duration: 12-week intensive

Enrollment: Rolling, cohort-based

Format: Virtual 1:1 + group sessions

Authorship: ICMJE-compliant, earned not gifted

Certificate: Verifiable Research Completion Certificate upon program completion
