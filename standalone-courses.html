Standalone Courses

Take individual MD Scholars modules à la carte. Focused, practical, and built from the same curriculum our full-track scholars use.

Why standalone courses?

Not every student needs a full 12-week mentorship arc. Standalone courses let physicians, residents, medical students, and pre-meds learn a single high-leverage skill — IRB submission, statistics in R, manuscript writing — and apply it the same week. Buy what you need. Most modules are 1-3 hours and come with downloadable templates.
